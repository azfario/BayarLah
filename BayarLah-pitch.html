Key insight
BayarLah does not move money. It eliminates the admin work around money — which is where all the friction actually lives.
Architecture
Built to be production-ready from day one.
No payment rails = no heavy compliance. BayarLah is a workflow layer over DuitNow — users transfer outside the app, we just track and verify. This lets us ship fast, iterate, and scale without a payment license.
AI is used where it creates real value: Gemini-assisted receipt parsing and OCR-based proof matching — two tasks that are genuinely tedious for humans to do manually.
